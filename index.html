My research interests focus on machine learning and reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on building models that are interpretable and reliable in real-world settings. I am particularly interested in understanding model behavior, improving robustness, and designing learning systems that generalize well. I am also keen on exploring deep learning and natural language processing, especially for developing scalable and trustworthy AI systems.
